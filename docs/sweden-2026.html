Every municipality, washed in the colour of the party that took most votes there, with its result stacked over it.

    How to read it
    Stack
    one bubble per party, biggest share at the bottom
    Bubble
    area follows the votes cast for that party there, not the land
    Ground
    the winner's colour, rising with the votes cast there (as the square root, so a city is a hill), municipality borders inked and county borders wider
    Scale
    one voxel is one kilometre
    Fly
    drag to look; WASD and Space/C to move, Shift to sprint, wheel to approach; on touch, two fingers fly

    Preliminary count, Valmyndigheten.
